Seasons

Everything we make answers to a season.

A season sets a question. For as long as it runs, the stories we commission, the people we add to the directory and the objects we make are all answers to it. When the question is exhausted, the season closes.

This page is a scaffold. The OLMIKAYA seasonal framework was listed among the brand assets but did not arrive, and nothing on this page invents one. The four slots below are structural placeholders only — they are not proposed seasons, and their titles are deliberately bracketed so they cannot be mistaken for real ones. Supply the framework and this page fills in without any redesign.

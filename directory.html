The directory

People and places, kept on the record.

Makers, cooks, tailors, roasters, carvers and the rooms they work in. Nothing appears here because it paid to. Entries are added when we have been, more than once, and taken down when they stop being true.

Placeholder entries. Every entry below is invented to prove the layout. No real business, address or person is described, and nothing here has been visited or verified. Replace the whole list before publishing — a directory that carries our name has to be earned, entry by entry.

Nairobi

Three entries

001

A roastery in a garage

Single origin, one bag at a time, roasted to order on a Saturday morning. No seating, no menu, no sign.

Four entries

006

A house that cools itself

Built in 1962, oriented to the wind, and still twelve degrees cooler inside than out. Visits by arrangement.

Dar es Salaam

007

A tailor who will not rush

Three fittings, no exceptions. Turns away more work than he takes, and is entirely unapologetic about it.

Kigali

008

The carver of Stone Town doors

Fourth generation, same workshop, same chisels. Restoration mostly; new commissions once or twice a year.

Zanzibar

009

A coffee house with no takeaway cups

You drink it there or you do not drink it. The ceremony takes an hour and the hour is the point.

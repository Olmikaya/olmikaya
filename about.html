About

Ordinary Living Made Intentional.

OLMIKAYA is an independent East African editorial and lifestyle company. We publish a journal, keep a directory of people and places, and make a small number of objects with the people we write about.

The name is the argument. Most of life is ordinary, and the ordinary parts are the ones worth doing on purpose.

Ordinary life is worth experiencing intentionally.

Principles

How we work

We go more than once. A single visit produces a description; a second produces an understanding. Nothing enters the directory on one visit, and nothing is written up from a phone call.

We do not take payment for coverage. Not for a story, not for a directory entry, not for a mention. If that ever changes it will be stated on this page before it is done anywhere else.

We name the people who made the thing. Photographers, writers, tailors, cooks and carvers get credited by name, in the piece, not in a caption nobody reads.

We publish slowly. The work takes as long as it takes, and a thin month is better than a padded one.

How the site is made

Set in Ovo, which has a single weight and no italic, and Inter, which carries everything you actually read. Seven colours, no gradients, and as little JavaScript as the pages can get away with.

The whole system — palette, contrast rules, type scale and components — is documented and visible.

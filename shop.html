Method

How anything gets made

An object only exists because a story came first. We do not design a product and then find someone to make it; we write about someone, and occasionally that turns into something you can hold.

The maker is named on the object and paid a share, not a fee. Runs are small and finite. When a run is gone it is gone, and nothing is reissued to meet demand.

If we cannot make something better than what the maker already sells, we do not make it, and we point you at theirs instead.

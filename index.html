Ordinary Living Made Intentional

Ordinary life is worth experiencing intentionally.

OLMIKAYA is an independent East African editorial and lifestyle company. We pay close attention to the things that are not supposed to be remarkable — a Sunday table, a well-made door, the route a rider takes before the city wakes.

A journal, a directory of people and places, and — in time — a small number of objects. Published from Nairobi.

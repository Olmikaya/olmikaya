The house was finished in 1962 and has never had air conditioning installed. On
the afternoon we visited, the outside temperature was thirty-one degrees and
the sitting room was nineteen.

## Three decisions

Nothing about this is clever in the way that word is usually used. It is three
decisions, made early and then never compromised.

- The long face of the house points into the prevailing wind.
- The walls are thick enough to lag half a day behind the air outside.
- Every window has a roof over it, so light arrives without sun.

> We did not invent anything. We just did not undo what everybody already knew.

## What replaced it

The techniques did not disappear because they stopped working. They disappeared
because a machine arrived that made them optional, and optional things are the
first to be cut from a budget.

The house is a quiet argument that the machine solved a problem the building had
already solved, more cheaply, and for longer.
